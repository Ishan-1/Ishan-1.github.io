MAVIS Architecture Series

An engineering deep-dive series on designing and building MAVIS (My Awesome Virtual Intelligence Suite) — a local-first, self-modifying personal assistant inspired by JARVIS. These posts discuss the technical design, trade-offs, and lessons learned from compiling deterministic DAGs, synthesizing tools on-the-fly, enforcing OS policy gates, managing multi-tier memory, and building compute-bounded autonomous reflection.
